The colony protocol

One ant is nothing.
A colony rewrites
the ground.

Every system we ship follows the same loop an ant colony has been running for a hundred million years: sense the terrain, share the signal, act in parallel, and get better with every pass.

STEP 01

Perceive

Connect to the real sources of truth — APIs, databases, documents, events — with typed, permissioned access.

STEP 02

Reason

Plan, decompose and route. The right model for the right sub-task, with explicit budgets and stop conditions.

STEP 03

Act

Real actions against real systems: write, deploy, notify, transact — every step traced and reversible.

STEP 04

Learn

Outcomes feed evals, evals feed prompts and policies. The colony gets sharper without a rewrite.
